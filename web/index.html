Three things, then music.

  1. Switch on the Cardputer-ADV and connect a data-capable USB-C cable.
  2. Close any serial monitor, press Install and choose the USB JTAG/serial device.
  3. Wait for verification. The box restarts straight into the instrument.

No port? Switch the box off, hold G0 while reconnecting or switching it on, release G0, then try again.
