Three steps

  1. Switch on the Cardputer-ADV and connect a data-capable USB-C cable.
  2. Close any serial monitor, click Install and select the USB JTAG/serial device.
  3. Wait for verification and let the box restart.

No port? Switch it off, hold G0 while reconnecting or switching it on, release G0, then retry.
